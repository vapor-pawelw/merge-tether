# MergeTether icon

The icon uses a retro gouache/screen-print style: intertwined navy branches,
cream paper, muted teal and a coral merge node. It is an AI-generated illustration,
not a claim of hand-painted authorship. The asset was generated with Codex's
built-in image-generation tool and then resized for the macOS app-icon catalog.

- Master used by the README: [mergetether-icon.png](../images/mergetether-icon.png)
- App sizes: [AppIcon.appiconset](../../Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset)
- The menu-bar status glyph is drawn as a small vector: neutral in the system menu-bar color, or amber when a human action is waiting. Its shape follows the connected branches of the app icon.

The selected generation/edit prompt:

> Radically restyle this MergeTether macOS app icon as a distinctive handmade retro gouache / mid-century printed-paper icon. Keep only the conceptual idea of two branch paths tethered together, not the glossy rendering. Warm ivory/cream rounded-square tile, slightly irregular hand-painted dark ink-navy branch/tether strokes with visible brush edges, a small vermilion/coral hand-painted node, a little muted teal. Charming confident editorial illustration from a 1960s technical field notebook, flat colors, subtle paper grain and misregistered screen-print character. Less geometric perfection, more crafted warmth, still a bold simple symbol legible at 32px. No shiny 3D, no gradients, no glow, no tech-startup gloss. Recompose the paths freely into a memorable branching knot rather than a literal letter M. Square single 1024x1024 app icon, transparent outside rounded tile. No typography, no words, no letters, no watermark, no presentation sheet.
